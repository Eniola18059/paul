The story

From a consult desk to a registered firm

Under his leadership, Paultimate has grown from its early days as Paultimate Consult into a formally registered private limited company, now serving clients both within Nigeria and internationally.

The firm supports students and researchers with research assistance, ethical clearance processing, data analysis, and plagiarism checking — work that requires the same discipline and attention to detail as the academic research it supports.

Alongside this, Paultimate also renders professional printing, graphic design, web development, and internet services, giving clients a single, trusted point of contact across research, registration, and design work.
